Worm, Drake, Griffith, King Roland, the cave, the castle geometry, trees, clouds, stars, particles, scale/rock/sand/heart/sparkle
textures (drawn at runtime on `<canvas>`), and all sound effects and music (synthesized with WebAudio at runtime). No third-party models.
